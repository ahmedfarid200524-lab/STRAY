Wash & care
01 — Wash cold
Machine wash cold at 30°C / 86°F. Turn garment inside out before washing.

02 — Gentle cycle
Use a gentle cycle with similar colors. Avoid aggressive washing.

03 — Do not bleach
Do not use bleach or harsh chemical detergents.

04 — No high heat
Tumble dry low or air dry. Avoid high temperatures to protect the fabric and graphics.

05 — Do not iron prints
If ironing is necessary, turn the garment inside out and avoid direct heat on printed or embroidered areas.

06 — Hang or lay flat
Air dry whenever possible. Hang or lay flat to preserve the garment's shape.

Stray care: treat it well. Wear it hard. Keep it longer.
